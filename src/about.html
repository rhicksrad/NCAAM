About

A fan-made scorer's table for men's college basketball. No account, no paywall, no API key.

What this is

Field House is Ryan Hicks's college hoops side project: scores, polls, standings, every Division I team, and a lab of charts that exist because they were fun to build. It is a static site. There is no server of ours between you and the data.

Where the numbers come from

  • Live scores, polls, standings, rosters and box scores come straight from ESPN's public JSON feeds, read by your browser. They need no key. They are also undocumented, so if a page looks odd, that feed probably changed shape.
  • A saved snapshot of those feeds is baked in every time the site deploys. Pages paint from the snapshot first and swap in live numbers a moment later. If ESPN doesn't answer, you keep the snapshot and an amber note tells you how old it is.
  • Player stat lines (2024-25 and earlier) were collected from Sports Reference.
  • Efficiency history (2013 to 2025) comes from Bart Torvik's T-Rank archive.
  • Arena locations and elevations, mascot categories and the cats-versus-dogs ledger were compiled by hand from school releases and public records.

How it got here

The first version ran on a paid Ball Don't Lie subscription behind a Cloudflare Worker. When the subscription ended, the site was rebuilt to stand on free public sources and its own archives. Earlier versions were built with OpenAI Codex; this one was rebuilt with Claude. The floor, scoreboard and chalkboard art was generated with Codex.

The fine print
